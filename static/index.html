About BallotAxis - Problem We Solve

The Problem: Millions of eligible Indian voters lack critical awareness about voter registration, the EVM voting process, and candidate research. This gap in civic education leads to lower participation and misinformation.

Our Mission: To bridge this gap through AI-powered civic education. BallotAxis provides personalized, non-partisan guidance to help every citizen move From Awareness to Action.
