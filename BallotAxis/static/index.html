Election Process Path 📅

📢

1. Announcement

ECI announces the schedule & Model Code of Conduct begins.

📋

2. Nominations

Candidates file papers, affidavits, and wait for scrutiny.

📣

3. Campaigning

Rallies and public meetings happen until 48 hours before polling.

🗳️

4. Polling Day

Citizens cast their votes using EVM and VVPAT.

🏆

5. Results

Votes are counted, winners declared, and government is formed.
